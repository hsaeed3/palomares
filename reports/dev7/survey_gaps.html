Coverage gaps and uncertain items

  • No Mapillary imagery: 2nd St, 3rd St, G/H/I/J St, L St, Vallejo St, the Commons/Terraces, Mission Blvd_4, Paloma Rd, and Niles Blvd east of s=520. Rows there are OSM street furniture plus OSM footprints with LiDAR heights (building.town_generic, facade "unverified"). NAIP was not used in this pass.
  • Sunol Main St: only 3 usable frames near s=238-292. The depot, crossings and shops are placed from OSM, and the 2023 frame confirms the depot and picket fence.
  • Niles depot area: the Niles Depot Museum, the SP freight station and the NCR Niles platform are 70-80 m from Niles Blvd and appear on no frame. They are placed from OSM, with the style taken from a standard SP depot (conf med).
  • Flasher text panel at NCR s=1824 R: the text is unreadable at 1024 px. The WB flasher at s=2318 is placed on the WB right side (L) by eye, but Mapillary triangulated it on R.
  • Guardrails: 24 LiDAR guardrail candidates have no matching by-eye run and are left as status=todo. Many look like vegetation. A dense frame check is needed before building them.
  • 2018-only map features on Niles Canyon Rd (re-imaged in 2023) are conf low and may no longer exist.
  • Map-feature signs carry no facing direction. dir is inferred from the side (R=nb, L=sb), and positions are within about 5-10 m.
