## Fixes applied in the pipeline (dev4)

- Kerbs (track_*): placed from the racing line of each direction (kerbs.py `line_rows`): apex only where the line's tyre is within 0.6 m of the inside edge while turning toward it, exit only after an apex on the other side; none at driveways (8 m), junctions (15 m), surveyed aprons, steep-drop insides or drop-off / steep-drop exits; run-off only where the line runs to <= 0.45 m of the edge and the ground is flat.
- Brake boards (21b): at 150/100/50 m before the turn-in of the 8 heaviest braking zones of the speed profile per direction; moved to >= 3.2 m past the shoulder (beyond the run-off) wherever they would sit in an exit / run-wide zone.
- Event barriers (21b): unprotected steep drops ranked by racing-line exposure (speed where the drop is on the outside of the line or the line runs within 0.6 m of it, either direction); slow / inside-only drops (< 70 km/h) are no longer barriered.
- Terrain holes (21d `fill_holes`): the open terrain edges near the road (gaps between terrain and the road end rows at s=0 and s=end) are closed along the road end row (1GRASS_hole_fill); sliver triangles on the collinear end row dropped (23_qa 3c).
- Kerbs: max_above_shoulder_m 0.05 -> 0.012 (kerb top was up to 79-92 mm above a falling verge; now <= ~41 mm) and kerb width clipped to the shoulder (no overhang onto terrain / cut banks: the 0.2-0.57 m kerb/terrain steps in 23_qa 3a).
- AI splines stop 6 m inside the road ends (they touched the 1WALL_start / 1WALL_end caps).
- Walls (21d): 'away from the road' for wall thickening measured from the centreline, not 1ROAD vertices (with wide shoulders the fence line sat on road vertices and whole collision boxes were pushed 0.3 m out: qa_collisions chainlink s=3500, guardrail 3490, fences/gate s=13625-14385).
- Race assets: grandstand site search penalises ground height off road level (summit stand was perched on the cut bank), spectator areas skip run-wide zones of exit kerbs, ROAD CLOSED barricades skipped where a surveyed gate/chain-link closes the mouth.
- Road profile (geom.Road): local smoothing where grade changes > 0.6 %/m (23_qa 3d spike s=7866), <= 4 cm change.
- Draw calls (22): lines merged per 500 m, edge strips L+R per 500 m, kerbs per 1 km.
- Ride model (qa_drive): vertical accel from a quarter-car (300/40 kg, 45 kN/m, 260 kN/m tyre, 0.15 m patch) instead of the raw 0.1 m contact-path 2nd derivative (30 g artefacts from mesh facets); bumps vs real crests separated on a 3 m-smoothed profile (crest_unload).
- 23_qa 3a: shoulder seams hidden under a kerb no longer count as steps (only boundaries sticking up out of the surface beneath); nb / sb kerbs are checked separately (never loaded together). 4_package timing: only road_summit's kn5 (the union let the last layout's gates win).
- Event barriers (21b): up to 36 exposed drops, 150 m spacing per side (was 18, any side). Marshal posts: every fast blind crest (sight < 120 m at > 110 km/h) first, up to 12 posts.
- Summit grandstand (race_assets): hard cap of 1.2 m between stand ground and road edge, search widened to +-150 m of the finish.
- fill_holes (21d): sliver threshold 5e-3 (float32 in the kn5 flipped 5 near-zero-area triangles: 23_qa 3c).
